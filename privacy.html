Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how The Cube (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use The Cube mobile application, the website at thecubelife.in, and related services (collectively, the “Service”). The Cube is your AI Health Companion for organizing family health records, reminders, insights, and emergency information.

1. Who we are

The Cube helps families securely store, organize, search, and review medical documents and related health metadata (lab values, visits, medications, vaccinations, billing, emergency card, and reminders). For privacy questions and grievances, please contact Devashish Sharma, the grievance officer, at devashish@thecubelife.in.

If you are in India, this notice describes how we handle personal data in connection with the Service and is intended to support our obligations under applicable data-protection law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP).

2. Information we collect

2.1 Account & profile

  • Email, phone (if used for authentication), and display name
  • Family member profiles you create (name, relationship, demographics you enter, and contacts)
  • Authentication identifiers from Supabase Auth and optional Google or Apple sign-in when enabled

2.2 Health & medical content you upload or enter

  • Medical documents (PDFs, images) and filenames
  • Extracted text, OCR, and AI-structured fields (labs, prescriptions, diagnoses, visits, bills, vaccinations, and vitals)
  • Emergency card fields (allergies, conditions, medications, blood group, emergency contacts, and insurance notes)
  • Medicine reminders and notification schedules
  • Optional ABHA / ABDM-linked data only if that feature is enabled and you consent

2.3 Billing

  • Subscription plan, status, invoices, payment method type, and transaction identifiers
  • Billing contact details you provide at checkout (name, email, phone, and address)
  • Payments are processed by Cashfree, our only payment gateway. UPI and card details are collected by Cashfree; the app never stores your card details

2.4 Device & technical

  • App version and basic device and operating-system information
  • Push or local notification tokens when reminders are enabled
  • Security logs necessary to operate authentication and prevent abuse

We do not sell your personal data.

3. How we use information

  • Provide the vault, search (including content search), insights, Your AI Health Companion, emergency card, and reminders
  • Process subscriptions and invoices
  • Improve reliability and security
  • Comply with law and enforce these Terms
  • Process document text through server-side AI providers under our control when AI features are used; do not paste secrets into the client

4. Legal bases / consent (high level)

  • Contract or service delivery for account and vault features
  • Consent where required, such as optional AI processing disclosures, marketing, or ABHA linking
  • Legitimate interests for security and fraud prevention, balanced against your rights

You may withdraw optional consents where applicable; some features will stop working without the related processing.

5. Sharing / subprocessors

We may share data with:

  • Supabase for authentication, database, and storage under our project configuration and access controls
  • Cashfree, our payment gateway, for checkout and subscription payments
  • AI vendors through server-side proxies when AI features are used
  • Professional advisers or authorities when legally required

We require processors to protect data appropriately. Your information may be processed in India or other countries where our service providers operate. We use appropriate contractual, technical, and organizational safeguards and handle international transfers as permitted by applicable law.

6. Retention

We retain account and vault data while your account is active and as needed for subscriptions, disputes, and legal obligations. You may request deletion (see §8). Backups may persist for a limited period.

7. Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS), access controls, and row-level security patterns. No method is 100% secure; please use a strong password and protect your device.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, export, or restriction of processing. For privacy requests, legal notices, or deletion requests, contact support@thecubelife.in or use an available account-deletion flow.

Children: The Cube is not directed at children under 13 (or under 18 if a higher age gate applies). A parent or guardian may create and manage a child’s family profile and records where permitted by law and is responsible for having the required authority and consent. Do not create an account for a child below the applicable age without parent or guardian involvement.

9. Medical data sensitivity

Health records are sensitive. We design features for private family use. Insights, scores, AI summaries, and extracted values may be wrong or incomplete — see the Medical Disclaimer.

10. Changes

We may update this Policy. Material changes will be reflected by a new “Last updated” date and may require renewed in-app acceptance.

11. Governing law

This Policy is governed by the laws of India. The courts at Pune, Maharashtra have exclusive jurisdiction, subject to mandatory consumer protections.

12. Contact

For privacy questions and grievances, please contact Devashish Sharma, the grievance officer, at devashish@thecubelife.in.

For support, legal notices, or deletion requests, contact support@thecubelife.in.
