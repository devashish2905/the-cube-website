Medical disclaimer

The Cube helps you organize family medical records and related reminders, and offers an AI Health Companion for informational summaries. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Insights, health scores, AI summaries, and extracted data may be incomplete or inaccurate. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional. In an emergency, contact local emergency services — do not rely solely on the in-app Emergency Card.

Using The Cube does not create a doctor–patient relationship with us. Medicine reminders can fail (device offline, OS limits, wrong schedule) and do not replace clinician instructions.
