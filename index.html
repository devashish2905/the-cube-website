Why The Cube

Built for real family health paperwork — not clinical claims.

Clinics hand you PDFs. Pharmacies text doses. Parents juggle kids’ vaccines and grandparents’ prescriptions. The Cube is a family medical vault: upload, search, remind, and share context within your household — so the right document is there when you need it.

Important

Medical disclaimer

The Cube helps you organize family medical records and related reminders. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Insights, health scores, AI summaries, and extracted data may be incomplete or inaccurate. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional. In an emergency, contact local emergency services — do not rely solely on the in-app Emergency Card.

Using The Cube does not create a doctor–patient relationship with us. Medicine reminders can fail (device offline, OS limits, wrong schedule) and do not replace clinician instructions.
